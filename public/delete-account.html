Buck the Duck — account and data deletion

You can delete your Buck the Duck account at any time. It takes effect straight away and cannot be undone.

Sign in and delete my account

How to delete it

  • In the app (Android or web): open your profile (your picture, top right), then tap Delete account and confirm.
  • From this page: use the button above. Sign in if asked, then confirm in the dialog that opens.
  • By email: if you can't sign in, email hello@bucktheduck.site from the address on your account, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email address and password, or your Google sign-in link)
  • Your profile: name, username, role, school and profile picture
  • Your quiz history and scores
  • Your calendar events and reminders
  • Your friends list, friend requests and messages, both sent and received
  • Live rooms you host and your seats in other rooms
  • Diagram images you uploaded

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers once deletion finishes. StudyPacks saved only in your browser or on your phone stay there until you delete them yourself or clear the app's storage. Rate-limit counters expire on their own within hours. Material sent to our AI provider to write questions is not stored by us beyond a cache of up to 24 hours.
